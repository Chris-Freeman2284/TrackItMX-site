Privacy Policy

TrackItMX privacy, in plain language.

This policy covers the current TrackItMX beta and launch-ready website surfaces. It is written to explain the kinds of data the app may handle, why that happens, and how people can contact you about privacy or deletion requests.

Last updated May 13, 2026

Review and update this page whenever the app’s data practices materially change.

Privacy contact trackitmx.beta@gmail.com

Use this inbox for privacy, deletion, and data-access requests.

1. Overview

TrackItMX is built around rider utility, not spam funnels.

TrackItMX is designed to help riders navigate to trails, record rides, review ride data, and optionally use cloud-backed features like group ride, public sharing, analytics, or restore flows. Core product direction is local-first where practical.

2. Data we may handle

Current beta data categories.

  • Location data used for navigation, mapping, ride recording, and group ride presence.
  • Ride telemetry and saved ride details such as timestamps, distance, speed, and route traces.
  • Group ride room data, rider aliases, and live presence when cloud-backed live features are used.
  • Optional watch and health-related signals such as heart rate or active energy if you enable those permissions.
  • Basic app usage and product-health analytics related to app sessions and feature reliability.
  • Support emails or other information you send when you contact TrackItMX.
3. Why we use it

We use data to make the product work and improve trust.

  • To show maps, compute routes, and support location-aware trail features.
  • To record, save, reopen, and analyze rides.
  • To power cloud-backed features such as live rooms, public trail posts, and restore flows.
  • To improve reliability and understand failures in recording, watch, group ride, or navigation features.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and beta feedback messages.
4. Cloud services

Some features rely on third-party infrastructure.

TrackItMX currently uses Firebase services for parts of authentication, cloud storage, live room, public sharing, analytics, and restore-related infrastructure. Navigation and mapping features may also rely on Apple-provided platform services. Optional health access is mediated through Apple HealthKit. The public website may also use website analytics tooling if TrackItMX enables it later.

5. Anonymous or optional identity

Not every feature requires a visible account profile.

Some cloud features may use anonymous backend identity or lightweight identifiers so the feature can work without forcing full account setup. Public sharing, group ride, or restore flows may still require enough identity context to link activity safely to the right rider or device.

6. Public sharing

If you choose to share, other riders may see that shared content.

If you post public ride results, join a live room, or otherwise choose to share information with other riders, selected ride details, aliases, route previews, or live presence data may be visible to others in that feature context.

7. Health and watch data

Optional by permission.

If you enable supported watch or HealthKit features, TrackItMX may read limited health-related data such as heart rate or active energy to support ride context or live safety-adjacent beta features. If you do not grant those permissions, the app should not have access to that data.

8. Retention and security

Retention varies by feature.

Some ride and settings data may remain on-device until you remove the app or delete that data. Cloud-backed content may remain in backend systems long enough to support the feature you used, debugging, abuse prevention, or restore behavior. TrackItMX should not sell personal ride data as a business model.

9. Website analytics

The public site can measure visits and performance.

If TrackItMX enables website analytics, the site may measure aggregated traffic, page views, referrers, or page performance to understand how people use the public website. This website package is prepared for optional analytics setup, but metrics are not active unless TrackItMX turns them on.

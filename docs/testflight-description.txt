TrackItMX iPhone beta is live.

Current beta focus:
- Ride recording accuracy
- Start, stop, pause, save, and restore behavior
- Group ride rooms, live presence, and syncing
- Apple Watch companion behavior
- CarPlay navigation behavior, where available
- Anything that feels confusing during a real ride

Use whatever off-road setup you have: bike, truck, SxS, or ATV.

Please send feedback that includes:
- What you were doing
- What device you used
- Whether Watch or CarPlay was involved
- What went wrong or felt confusing
- What worked well
- Any steps that could help reproduce the issue

Don’t baby it. Ride it, test it, and send the real notes.

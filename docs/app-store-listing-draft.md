## Description

TrackItMX helps off-road riders get to the trail, record rides, review sessions, remember setup context, and ride smarter with their crew.

Record the ride from start to finish, then reopen the session with the route, timing, movement, and ride context that matter. Use Group Ride rooms to keep connected riders visible when network conditions allow, share spectator links with people who do not have the iPhone app, and keep garage/setup notes close to the ride instead of scattered across screenshots and memory.

TrackItMX is built for practical ride use:

- Off-road trail lookup and route context
- Ride recording for trail and lap-style sessions
- Post-ride review and session history
- Group Ride rooms and live rider presence
- Web spectator links for active rooms
- Apple Watch companion ride context where available
- CarPlay navigation support where available
- Garage and setup memory
- Ride Coach and Mechanic guidance based on available ride and setup context

TrackItMX is not emergency rescue infrastructure, professional instruction, medical advice, or a substitute for your OEM service manual. Always ride within your limits, follow local rules, and use local emergency services in a real emergency.

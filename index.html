Trust and safety

Your ride data should work for you, not become a spam funnel.

TrackItMX may handle sensitive ride information like location, route traces, telemetry, Group Ride presence, spectator visibility, and optional Watch or health-related signals. Those choices should be clear, permission-based, and easy to question.

  • Location and telemetry are used to power navigation, recording, review, and Group Ride features.
  • Watch and health-related signals are optional and permission-based.
  • Cloud-backed features such as group rooms, sharing, restore, and backup may require network access.
  • TrackItMX is not emergency rescue infrastructure. In a real emergency, use local emergency services.

FAQ

Quick answers before you ride.

Where do I download TrackItMX?

Use the Get TrackItMX buttons on this site. Once the public App Store listing is connected, those buttons point directly to the TrackItMX App Store page.

What makes TrackItMX different?

TrackItMX is focused on the whole ride loop: get to the trail, record the session, review what happened, remember setup context, and improve next time. Navigation matters, but the long-term product is ride memory and ride review for off-road riders.

Does TrackItMX work without cell service?

Core location and recording behavior is built with low-service riding in mind. Cloud-backed features like live Group Ride rooms, spectator links, sharing, backup, restore, and some syncing still need network access.

Can someone follow a ride without TrackItMX installed?

Yes. Spectator links can open a live Group Ride room in the browser. Private shared ride links can also open a saved ride recap on the website after the rider chooses to share that ride.

How does TrackItMX track my ride?

With your permission, TrackItMX uses device location, timing, movement, saved ride events, and optional Watch signals to record the session and build a clearer post-ride review.

How do Ride Coach and Mechanic work?

TrackItMX uses available ride and garage context to surface grounded coaching and setup guidance. The goal is useful direction from your own ride history, not vague AI hype or professional mechanical advice.

What does TrackItMX track?

Depending on the features and permissions you use, TrackItMX may handle location, route traces, ride telemetry, timestamps, speed, distance, device motion signals, Group Ride presence, optional Watch or health-related signals, app reliability analytics, and support messages.

Is TrackItMX for emergencies?

No. TrackItMX is a ride app, not emergency rescue infrastructure. In a real-world emergency, contact local emergency services.

Is Android coming?

Android is planned after the iPhone launch foundation is stable enough to expand well.

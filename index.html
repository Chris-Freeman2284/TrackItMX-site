Why TrackItMX

Built around trust, not filler.

If the app loses a ride, saves nonsense, or makes the crew map feel fake, nothing else matters. TrackItMX is being shaped around the parts riders notice first: whether the route makes sense, whether the ride saves cleanly, whether the review tells the truth, whether the crew map stays believable, and whether the app remembers useful setup context after the dust settles.

Trust and safety

Your ride data should work for you, not become a spam funnel.

TrackItMX may handle sensitive ride information like location, route traces, telemetry, group ride presence, and optional Watch or health-related signals. The beta should make those choices clear, permission-based, and easy to question.

  • Location and telemetry are used to power navigation, recording, review, and group ride features.
  • Watch and health-related signals are optional and permission-based.
  • Cloud-backed features such as group rooms, sharing, restore, and backup may require network access.
  • TrackItMX is not emergency rescue infrastructure. In a real emergency, use local emergency services.

FAQ

Quick answers before you ride.

Is TrackItMX on the App Store yet?

Not yet. TrackItMX is currently available through Apple TestFlight for iPhone beta testers.

What makes TrackItMX different?

TrackItMX is focused on the whole ride loop: get to the trail, record the session, review what happened, remember setup context, and improve next time. Navigation matters, but the long-term product is ride memory and ride review for off-road riders.

Does TrackItMX work without cell service?

Core navigation and recording flows are being built with low-service riding in mind. Cloud-backed features like live group rooms, spectator links, sharing, backup, restore, and some syncing still need network access. Weak-service Group Ride is active beta work, not a promise we are done hardening.

Can someone follow a ride without TrackItMX installed?

Yes. Shared spectator links can open on the web, so a non-iPhone user can still follow room presence and live rider context from a browser when the room is active and connected.

Are coaching and mechanic features fully public yet?

Not yet. Assistant and Mechanic are being hardened carefully, and broader automated ride analysis stays controlled until it clears stronger usefulness, accuracy, and confidence gates.

What does TrackItMX track?

Depending on the features and permissions you use, TrackItMX may handle location, route traces, ride telemetry, timestamps, speed, distance, group ride presence, optional Watch or health-related signals, app reliability analytics, and support messages.

Is TrackItMX for emergencies?

No. TrackItMX is a ride app, not emergency rescue infrastructure. In a real-world emergency, contact local emergency services.

Is Android coming?

Android is planned after the iPhone beta foundation is stable enough to expand well.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is TrackItMX on the App Store yet?

Not yet. TrackItMX is still in active beta. Use the beta email for access or launch updates.

Do I need an account to use TrackItMX?

The product direction is local-first. Some cloud features may use anonymous or optional account layers, but the goal is not to force signup before the app proves value.

Does TrackItMX work without cell service?

Some navigation and ride flows are built for low-service environments. Cloud-backed features still need network access.

What does the app actually track?

Depending on the features you use, TrackItMX may handle location, ride telemetry, saved ride details, optional watch or health signals, group ride presence, and basic usage analytics. The privacy page covers the current beta scope.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Email trackitmx.beta@gmail.com or open the support page for troubleshooting and help.

Support FAQ

Start here before emailing.

What devices does TrackItMX support?

TrackItMX is centered on iPhone, with Apple Watch companion flows and CarPlay beta work. Feature availability can vary depending on device, OS version, and whether the watch app is installed.

Do I need to create an account first?

Not for the core local-first direction. Some cloud-backed features may use anonymous backend identity or optional account layers, but TrackItMX is intended to earn that step rather than force it up front.

Can I use TrackItMX without cell service?

Core navigation and recording flows are designed with low-service riding in mind. Features like live rooms, public sharing, and cloud restore still depend on network availability.

How do I report a recording issue?

Email support with the trail or track name, whether you used phone-only or watch-linked recording, whether the app was in the background, and what you expected versus what happened.

How do group rides work?

Group Ride uses cloud-backed room features to create or join a live room and see rider presence. The experience depends on permission state, device connectivity, and whether the phone or watch is acting as the live source.

How do I ask for privacy help or data deletion?

Use the privacy page or email trackitmx.beta@gmail.com with enough detail for us to identify the request safely.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and where to look first.

Location + navigation

Trail distances or routing look wrong

Confirm that iPhone location permissions are enabled, let location settle before starting a route, and try again in a stronger signal area if the first fix was weak.

Ride recording

A ride did not save the way you expected

Note whether you were phone-only or watch-linked, whether the app went to the background, and whether the session was paused or interrupted. Include those details in the support email.

Apple Watch

The watch companion does not look right

Check that the watch is paired, the watch app is installed, and the phone stays nearby long enough for the bridge to settle if you are testing watch-linked live behavior.

Group Ride

Live rider presence looks stale or missing

Group Ride depends on permissions and connectivity. If the rider source is watch-led, temporarily keeping the phone nearby may help while the watch bridge refreshes.

Cloud backup

Restore or recovery is not working

Make sure the recovery code is entered carefully and that the restore attempt has network access. If it still fails, email support and mention that you were using the cloud backup flow.

Emergency note

TrackItMX is not emergency rescue infrastructure

If you have a real-world emergency, use local emergency services. TrackItMX support is for product help, not emergency response.
